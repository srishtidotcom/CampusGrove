Can't find the QR?
The metal plate is nailed at eye level on the trunk's south-facing side, or near the base on short trees.
📵
No camera access?
Use the "Enter Tree ID" tab and type the ID printed below the QR code (e.g. T001).
🧩
QR Tag Damaged?
Report a damaged tag via the tree's profile page or email trees@campus.edu
💡
Tip for Best Scan
Hold the phone 15–25 cm from the tag in shade. Angle it slightly to avoid glare on the metal plate.
