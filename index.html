📷 QR Scanning

Point, Scan, Discover

Each tree wears a weatherproof QR tag. Just scan it with any smartphone — no app needed.

1

Find the QR Tag

Look for the green weatherproof QR plate nailed at eye-level on the tree trunk.

2

Open the Scanner

Tap "Scan QR" on this website or use your phone's built-in camera to point at the code.

3

Discover the Tree

Instantly see the tree's full profile — age, health, care guide, and fascinating facts.

4

Report Issues

Notice something wrong? Submit a health report via the tree's profile page — it goes straight to the horticulture team.

🌱 Biodiversity

Local Roots, Global Visitors

Our campus hosts a diversity of tree species — native guardians and exotic ambassadors alike.

🇮🇳

Local / Native Trees

These trees evolved in India and are perfectly adapted to local soil, climate, and wildlife. They support native birds, bats, pollinators, and insects. Preserving them is key to campus biodiversity.

🌍

Exotic / Introduced Trees

These trees were introduced from other continents. While beautiful and valued, some grow aggressively. They're monitored for invasive behaviour and managed responsibly.
